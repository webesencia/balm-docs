Texture

A trail of cream drawn across the screen in the product color, with its texture, its skin feel and how to use it.

WhereOnly on product templates.

What it is for

Under the product details, show what the formula feels like. The section belongs to the product template and reads the product of the page.

How it works

The trail draws itself as the section comes into view; without JavaScript or with reduced motion it is drawn at once. Beside it: Subheading and the Texture line set large, Skin feel label and Skin feel; under it, How to use, the price and Add to bag. Connect the three texts to product metafields so each product shows its own: empty, Texture reads custom.texture and Skin feel reads custom.skin_feel. An empty text is not printed.

Content tips

  • Connect How to use to custom.how_to_use, a multi-line text metafield (see Metafields).
  • Choosing a variant with its own colors recolors the trail too.
