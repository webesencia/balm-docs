Texture

A photo of the product's texture, or its matter drawn in its color, with its texture, its skin feel and how to use it.

WhereOnly on product templates.

What it is for

Under the product details, show what the formula feels like. The section belongs to the product template and reads the product of the page.

How it works

Source on Automatic, the default, shows the texture photo of the product when it has one: the image of the custom.texture_image metafield (File, image), cut out, large and centered on the tinted paper with a soft shadow. As the section comes into view it slides in and spreads a little; without JavaScript or with reduced motion it stands still. A product without a photo, or the section with Source on Illustration, shows the illustration instead.

Material picks the illustration among five renderings, each clearly its own: Gel (a translucent dome with a sharp highlight and micro-bubbles), Cream (an opaque satin swirl with thick edges and soft peaks), Balm (a dense, waxy swatch with a light sheen), Fluid (a thin, wide spread with a liquid edge) and Serum (a drop running ahead of a glossy trail). On Automatic, the default, the section reads the texture metafield of the product (custom.texture), then the Texture line, and looks for the words gel, cream, balm, fluid or serum, in any of the theme's languages; without one it draws Cream. The color always comes from the product. The matter comes in as the section comes into view; without JavaScript or with reduced motion it is drawn at once. Beside it: Subheading and the Texture line set large, Skin feel label and Skin feel; under it, How to use, the price and Add to bag. Connect the three texts to product metafields so each product shows its own: empty, Texture reads custom.texture and Skin feel reads custom.skin_feel. An empty text is not printed.

Hide when the product has no texture, on by default, leaves the section out for a gift card and for a product with no texture and no texture photo (Gift wrapping on the demo); the theme editor shows a note instead.

Content tips

  • For the photo, create the product metafield custom.texture_image of type File, accepting images (see Metafields), and upload a PNG or WebP with a transparent background, about 2,000 px wide, the matter centered with some empty margin around it: the margin may pass under the texts, the matter stays between them.
  • Connect How to use to custom.how_to_use, a multi-line text metafield (see Metafields).
  • Write the texture of each product with one of the five words, for example Soft balm or Bouncy gel, and keep Material on Automatic: one template then serves the whole range.
  • Choosing a variant with its own colors recolors the matter too.
