Product page blocks

Balm Studio ships one product page, and every product uses it. The page wears the colors of the product, and metafields give each product its own cutout, name lines and texts, so one template serves the whole range.

How the page is built

The Product section has two sides. The scene shows the product name set large in layers around the cutout of the product, with Next product at the bottom: the name of the next product, which opens it. The scene is also the gallery: the product's other media follow the cutout, over the same name, and shoppers move through them with a swipe, a mouse drag (the pointer shows a hand over the scene), a trackpad, the arrow keys once the scene has the focus, or the two small arrows on each side of the counter (01 / 02), at the bottom right next to the zoom button; the arrows go round from the last media to the first. On phones the next media shows at the edge of the screen, to say that a swipe goes on. A product with one media shows no counter and no arrows. Choosing a variant brings its image: in the scene when the variant has its own cutout or no cutout is set, else on the variant's slide.

The column holds the blocks you add, remove and reorder in the theme editor (open Products in the page menu at the top of the editor). The section also accepts app blocks and the general theme blocks.

The whole page wears the colors of the product (see Tone on tone), and choosing a variant that has its own colors recolors it. Surface picks the ground of the section, Field by default. Sticky scene (desktop) keeps the scene in view while the column scrolls, until the end of the section; on phones the scene sits above the column.

Buy button styles

The Style setting of the Buy buttons block is Solid (the filled button of the theme, the default), Outline or Signature. Button height and Font weight are set on the block; the size and the border of every button are set in Theme settings, Buttons.

Weld to the packs menu joins the buy button to the Packs menu variant picker so the two read as one shape; it needs the Signature style. Turn on Weld to the buy button in the Variant picker block too.

The product under the header

Extend the background under the header runs the ground of the section up behind the header, which stays transparent at the top of the page. It needs Header behavior on Transparent at top in the Header and the Product section first on the page, as in the demo template. Whatever its Section width, the Product section keeps its background edge to edge; Section width moves its content only.

Show background illustration draws an image of your choice behind the scene, with its opacity, its width and its place set per device.

The accelerated checkout buttons

With Dynamic checkout buttons on, the wallet buttons (Shop Pay, PayPal, Google Pay, Apple Pay) show under the buy button. Which ones appear depends on the payment methods of your store and on the visitor's browser. The row is hidden while the selected variant is sold out and comes back with a variant that can be bought; a pre-order variant keeps it. Quick view and the sticky add to cart bar have no accelerated checkout buttons.

These buttons are drawn by Shopify. Balm Studio sets what Shopify allows from your buy button:

WhatFollows
HeightYour Button height, within Shopify's range of 25 to 55 px
CornersSquare, like the buy button
ShadowNone
Loading placeholderThe colors of the page, instead of Shopify's gray

No theme can change the brand color, the logo or the label of each button: Shop Pay stays purple and PayPal yellow on every Shopify store. Because Shopify caps the wallet height at 55 px, a Button height above 55 makes the buy button taller than the wallet row.

The heading has its alignment, its size on desktop and on mobile, and its weight; Inner padding and Space between rows (desktop) and (mobile) are set per device too. Open by default opens the row when the page loads: check it on the first row to show its content at once. To let only one row stay open, turn on One collapsible row open at a time in the Product section. When one product needs a different layout

Metafields let one template carry different colors, texts and images per product. Some settings cannot be connected to a metafield: sliders, checkboxes, dropdowns, the color scheme and Liquid code. When a product needs a different value for one of those, create a second template: in the theme editor, open Products in the page menu, choose Create template, base it on the default product template, then assign it to the product from its admin page, under Theme template.

Products that used an older template

Early versions of Balm Studio shipped extra product templates. They have been folded into the default one. A product still assigned to one of them falls back to the default product template and renders normally. To clear the setting, open the product in the admin and choose Default product under Theme template.
