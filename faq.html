FAQ

Answers to the questions merchants ask most about Balm.

Can I use Balm on more than one store?

A theme bought from the Shopify Theme Store is licensed for one store. To use Balm on another store, buy it again from that store. Trying the theme is free on any store.

How do I update Balm without losing my settings?

When an update is available, your theme library shows a notice. Shopify adds the new version as a separate copy that keeps your theme editor settings and content. Preview it, then publish it. Changes you made in the theme code are not carried over; see Custom code.

How do I make the header transparent over my hero?

  1. Make sure the first section of the page is a Hero, Slideshow, Video, Products showcase or Brand story hero, with Allow transparent header checked.
  2. In the Header section, check Transparent header over the first section.
  3. If your hero is dark, upload a light version of your logo in Logo image (transparent header), check Use different colors while transparent, and pick a Transparent header color scheme with light text.

A floating announcement bar over the same image can follow those colors: check Use the transparent header colors on the announcement bar in the Announcement bar section. See the white header example.

On the product page, the Product section can run its Product gradient under the transparent header: see Extend the gradient under the header.

Why does the product gradient not reach under the header?

Four conditions, all needed: Background mode of the Product section is Product gradient (metafields), Extend the background under the header is checked, the Header section has Transparent header over the first section turned on, and the Product section is the first section of the product template. A Solid announcement bar in full width stays above the gradient; a Floating or Glass bar lets it run underneath. If the header text is hard to read over a dark gradient, set Header colors at rest to Transparent header colors. See Extend the gradient under the header.

Why is there no country or language selector in my header?

The selectors only show when there is a choice to make. The country selector needs at least two countries or regions in Settings, Markets; the language selector needs at least two published languages in Settings, Languages, active in the visitor's market. Then check that Country and language selectors in the Header section is not set to Off. See Country and language selectors.

Why are there no filters on my collection pages?

Filters come from Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. Install it, add the filters you want (availability, price, product type, color and so on), then check that Enable filters is on in the Collection and Search sections. A filter only shows when the products of the page have values for it.

How do I set up a mega menu?

Create your main menu in your admin first. Then, in the Header section, add a Mega menu block and type the exact title of a top level menu item in Linked menu item, for example Shop. Add Link column, Image, Products, Promo or Banner blocks inside it. The panel opens when a shopper hovers that item. See Mega menu blocks.

How do I give each product its own background color?

Create two product metafield definitions of type Color, Background color (custom.background_color) and Background color end (custom.background_color_end), and fill them on each product. Then set Background mode of the Product section to Product gradient (metafields): there is nothing to connect, and one color alone gives a flat background. For a solid color or a radial halo, connect the metafield to the setting instead. Both ways are on Metafields.

How do I make all headings, or all text, larger?

Use Heading scale and Body scale in Theme settings, Typography. They multiply every size of the theme, the ones set in sections and blocks included, from 80 to 130%. Changing each section's sizes one by one is only worth it for a single element. See Type sizes.

Why is there no text behind my product image?

The text of Text behind cutouts only shows behind PNG and WebP images; JPG images, videos and 3D models never get it. Check that Show on desktop or Show on mobile is on for the device you are looking at, and that the first media of the gallery is a PNG or WebP image when Apply to is First image only. With Text on Product tagline, a product whose custom.tagline is empty shows no text. See Text behind cutout images.

Why does the spinning badge not show on some products?

The ring prints the product's custom.badge_label metafield and has no text of its own: a product with no value shows no ring. Fill the metafield on that product (see Badge label, step by step). With Next to the title, the page also needs a Title block.

Where are Balm spotlight, Lifestyle strip and the Sticky add to cart block?

They belonged to preview versions of Balm and were removed before release, together with the free text of the spinning badge.

  • Balm spotlight, the second version of the Product section: its gradient and its spinning badge are now the Product gradient background mode and the positions of the Spinning badge block, on the one Product section.
  • Lifestyle strip, two photos side by side: the section has been removed.
  • Sticky add to cart block: the bar is the Sticky add to cart section, placed below the Product section in the product template.
  • Spinning badge text: the ring reads the product's custom.badge_label metafield only.

Why can I not connect a setting to my metafield?

Three usual causes. The definition does not exist yet: create it in Settings, Custom data first. The type does not match: a color setting only accepts a Color metafield, an image setting a File metafield. Or the definition was created for the wrong resource: product page settings need a Products definition, other sections a Shop definition. The help text of each connectable setting names the exact definition it expects.

How do I add product reviews and star ratings?

Install a reviews app that writes Shopify's standard review metafields (most do). Balm's Rating block, the ratings on product cards and quick view read them automatically once reviews come in. If the app offers an app block for its review list, add it to the product page from the block picker.

How do I add a size guide or ingredients list to product pages?

Create a page with the content in Online Store, Pages. On the product page, add a Popup block (a link that opens a dialog) or a Collapsible row, and set Content comes from to A page. For content that differs per product, connect the block's Content to a rich text metafield instead.

Can I change the color of the Shop Pay or PayPal buttons?

No theme can. The accelerated checkout buttons are drawn by Shopify with the brand colors of each payment provider. Balm matches everything Shopify allows: height, corner radius, spacing and the loading placeholder. See The accelerated checkout buttons.

How do I show a free shipping progress bar?

Open the Cart drawer section, check Show the reward progress bar and type the amount in Reward threshold. The bar only displays progress: create the matching free shipping rate or automatic discount in your admin, and keep the two amounts the same, or buyers will be promised something checkout does not apply.

Why does the Buy it now button disappear on some variants?

The accelerated checkout buttons (Buy it now, Shop Pay, PayPal and the other wallets) are hidden while the selected variant is sold out, since it cannot be bought, and come back when the shopper picks a variant in stock. A variant that sells as a pre-order can be bought, so it keeps them. See The accelerated checkout buttons.

How do I offer pre-orders?

Set the variant to continue selling when out of stock in the product admin. At 0 stock its buttons read Pre-order, or the label you set in Theme settings, Pre-order. To take a deposit or charge later, use a pre-order app. See Pre-order.

How do I translate my store?

Add and publish your languages in Settings, Languages. Balm's own texts are already translated into French, German, Italian and Spanish. Translate what you typed in the editor with the Translate & Adapt app. See Translations.

The newsletter popup does not appear. What should I check?

  • Enable popup is checked in the Newsletter popup section, and the section is on the template you are viewing (by default, the home page only).
  • You closed it earlier: it stays hidden for the number of days in Reappear after. Test in a private window.
  • The Open delay has not passed yet.

My speed score dropped after I turned on the page loader. Why?

A loader covers the page while it loads, which speed tools measure as a slower page. Set Page loader to First visit only, the recommended setting, or turn it off. Every page shows it on each page view and has the largest effect.
