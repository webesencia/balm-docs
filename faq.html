FAQ

Answers to the questions merchants ask most about Balm Studio.

Can I use Balm Studio on more than one store?

A theme bought from the Shopify Theme Store is licensed for one store. To use Balm Studio on another store, buy it again from that store. Trying the theme is free on any store.

How do I update Balm Studio without losing my settings?

When an update is available, your theme library shows a notice. Shopify adds the new version as a separate copy that keeps your theme editor settings and content. Preview it, then publish it. Changes you made in the theme code are not carried over; see Custom code.

How do I give each product its own color?

Create the two color metafields, custom.background_color (the dark color) and custom.background_color_end (the light color), then fill them on each product. See Metafields, The two colors.

Why does a product show the violet house colors?

The product has no colors of its own yet: fill its two color metafields. Until then it takes the house pair of Theme settings, Tone, the violet of the preset.

Why is my product shown as a square photo instead of standing upright?

It has no cutout. Upload a PNG of the product on a transparent background, cropped tight, in its custom.cutout metafield. See Metafields, The cutout.

How do I show star ratings?

Install a reviews app that writes Shopify's standard rating metafields, or fill custom.rating and custom.rating_count on each product yourself. To show them on the collection tiles too, turn on Show the product rating in the Collection section. See Product ratings.

How do I set up the Shop mega menu?

In your main-menu, put your category collections under Shop. In the Header, add a Mega menu block, type Shop in Linked menu item and choose the Index layout. The categories show with their number of products, and pointing at a product previews it in its colors.

Why are there no filters on my collection pages?

Filters come from Shopify's free Search and Discovery app: install it and choose the filters there. Then keep Enable filters on in the Collection section.

How do I show the categories above a collection?

Create a menu with the handle shop-filters: All, linked to /collections/all, then one link per collection. The Collection section of the demo already points to it in its Menu setting.

How do I give an article its color and its reading time?

Fill the article's custom.product metafield with a product: the article takes its colors and shows its tile. Fill custom.reading_time with the minutes; without it the time is worked out from the length of the text. See Metafields, Articles.

Why does Heading weight not make my headings bolder?

Gloock, the heading font of the preset, has a single weight. The theme never fakes a bolder one, so every weight shows the same letters. Choose a heading font with several weights in Typography to use Heading weight.

Why does my header change color as I scroll?

The header takes the surface of the section under it, with its text kept readable, so it always belongs to what it sits on. There is nothing to set per section.

How do I set up gift wrapping?

Create a product for the wrapping, with its price. In Theme settings, Gift wrapping, turn on Offer gift wrapping and pick it in Wrapping product. The checkbox then shows in the cart.

How do I show a free shipping progress bar?

In Theme settings, Cart, keep Show the free shipping bar on and type the amount in Free shipping threshold. The bar only displays progress: create the matching free shipping rate in your admin and keep the two amounts the same.

How do I offer a discount on a routine?

The Routine builder shows the real total of the chosen products. Create the discount in your admin, under Discounts, and describe it in the section's Offer text. See The routine discount.

Can I change the color of the Shop Pay or PayPal buttons?

No theme can. The accelerated checkout buttons are drawn by Shopify with the colors of each payment provider.

How do I offer pre-orders?

Set the variant to continue selling when out of stock in the product admin. At 0 stock its buttons read Pre-order, or the label you set in Theme settings, Pre-order. To take a deposit or charge later, use a pre-order app.

How do I hide the breadcrumbs on one page?

Set Breadcrumbs on this page to Hide in the main section of that page, or turn them off for the whole store in Theme settings, Breadcrumbs.

How do I translate my store?

Add and publish your languages in Settings, Languages. Balm Studio's own texts are already translated into French, German, Italian and Spanish. Translate what you typed in the editor with Shopify's Translate and Adapt app.
