Features

The features that span several sections or the whole store: what they do, where to turn them on and what to know before you rely on them.

Quick view

Quick view opens a product in a sheet from its tile, so shoppers choose a variant and add it to the cart without leaving the page. The sheet wears the colors of the product and shows its gallery, its price, its rating, the same variant picker as the product page, the add to cart button, pickup availability, a short description and a link to the full product page.

  1. Open a section that shows tiles: Collection, Featured collection, Search, Recently viewed or Lookbook.
  2. Set Quick view to Show on hover or Always show. The tile then shows the word Quick view.
  3. In Theme settings, Quick view, choose what the sheet shows.

Without JavaScript the word opens the product page.

Product badges

Four badges can appear on tiles, in quick view, in Compare products and on the product page. Each states a fact of the product. Their position and the text of the Sale badge are set in Theme settings, Product badges.

  • Sale: when the variant shown has a compare-at price above its price. It reads Sale, the percentage off or the amount saved.
  • Sold out: when the product can no longer be bought. It replaces Sale and New.
  • New: off by default; on, for products with a tag (new on the demo) or created in the last days you set.
  • Custom: the text of the product's custom.badge_label metafield.

Sections that show tiles have a Badges setting (All, Automatic only, Custom only or None). On the product page, the Badge block and Badges on media of the Product section show them.

Ratings and swatches on tiles

In the Collection section, Show the product rating prints the rating of each product above its name, with the number of reviews, read as on the product page (see Product ratings). Show color swatches draws the values of the first option named in Swatch option name (Color, Colour or Couleur by default) with the swatch saved on each value in your admin, or as a word when a value has none. Each swatch is a link to its variant, up to Maximum swatches before "+N", in the Swatch size and Swatch shape you choose. Both settings are off by default.

Grid and Index views

The Collection section shows its products as a grid of tiles in their colors, or as an index: rows of names set large, each previewing its product as you point at it. Default view on desktop and Default view on mobile choose the view; Let visitors switch between Grid and Index adds the two words to the tools, and the choice is kept while the visitor browses. See Collection.

Column selector

The Collection section can let shoppers choose how many products they see per row. Columns (desktop) and Columns (mobile) set the starting layout; Let visitors change the column count (desktop) and (mobile) add the buttons. Each device keeps its own choice for the rest of the visit. Both are off by default.

Pagination

The Collection and Search sections have a Pagination setting with three modes: Page numbers, Load more button, or Infinite scroll, where the next page is added as the shopper reaches the end of the grid and a Load more button takes over after three pages so the footer stays reachable.

Header colors

The header takes the surface of the section under it, Field, Deep, Paper, a color scheme or an image, with its text and the announcement bar kept readable. It hides on the way down and comes back on the way up. There is nothing to set per section. See Header.

Gift wrapping

Adds a gift wrapping checkbox, and an optional gift message, to the cart drawer and the cart page. Checking the box adds your wrapping product to the cart in quantity 1 and records the cart attribute Gift wrapping: Yes; the message is saved as the cart attribute Gift message. Both appear on the order in your admin.

  1. Create a product for the wrapping, for example Gift wrapping, with its price and an image, available on the Online Store, and keep it out of your collections.
  2. In Theme settings, Gift wrapping, turn on Offer gift wrapping and pick it as the Wrapping product.
  3. Adjust the label, the help text, the gift message and where it shows.

The wrapping line stays at quantity 1, and a cart that holds only the wrapping is emptied.

Age verifier

A window that asks visitors to confirm their age before they browse the store, on every page of the online store, never at checkout.

  1. In Theme settings, Age verifier, turn on Enable the age verifier.
  2. Set the Minimum age. Fields left empty use the translated storefront text.
  3. Choose what happens when the visitor declines, and how long the answer is remembered.
  4. Add a background image if you like, and turn on Preview in the theme editor to see it while you edit.

The age verifier is a self-declaration, not an identity check: make sure it meets the rules that apply to your products where you sell.

Pre-order

A variant becomes a pre-order when its quantity is tracked, its stock is at 0 and Continue selling when out of stock is checked in the product admin. Its buttons then read Pre-order, or the Button label of Theme settings, Pre-order, and it keeps the accelerated checkout buttons. The Stock status block keeps its own text. The order is paid at checkout like any other: to take a deposit or charge later, use a pre-order app whose options show in the Purchase options block.

Combined listings

Combined listings group separate products into one listing, for example one product per size, on Shopify Plus stores with Shopify's Combined Listings app. On the product page, picking a value that belongs to another product of the listing switches the page to that product without a reload, keeping the values already chosen. In quick view, such a value loads that product into the sheet.

Lookbook

The Lookbook section turns a photo into a shop: each point on the image opens a product, with its quick add; on a phone, a tap opens it as a sheet. Add one Hotspot block per product and place it in percent of the image, desktop and mobile apart. With Layout on Image with product list, the products line up beside the image.

Compare products

The Compare products section sets up to five products side by side. Built in rows show the price, the availability, the vendor and the rating; each Row block you add reads one fact on every product, from a metafield you name or from a field of the product. Highlight differences marks the rows where products differ, and Mobile layout shows cards or a table that scrolls. See Metafields, Compare products rows.

Recently viewed

The product page records each product a visitor opens, in that visitor's own browser, and the Recently viewed section shows them, newest first. Nothing is sent to a server. The section takes no space until the visitor has a history. Exclude the current product and Maximum products shape the list.

Type sizes

Every text sits on one fluid type scale: a size is reached at 390 px wide for its mobile value and at 1440 px for its desktop value, and grows in a straight line in between. To make the whole store read larger or smaller, move Heading scale or Body scale in Theme settings, Typography: they multiply every size, those set in sections included. Product names and prices on tiles are set in Theme settings, Tiles.

Page width

Page width in Theme settings, Layout sets how wide the shopping pages grow: the product page and the collection and search pages. Standard, Wide, or Full width with margins, which fills the screen less the side margins. The other sections set their width with Section width.

Back to top

A Back to top link at the end of every page, just before the footer, set in Theme settings, Back to top. It works without JavaScript, and goes up at once for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

Translations

Balm Studio ships in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish: every storefront text of the theme and the theme editor itself, which follows the language of your admin. Add and publish your languages in Settings, Languages, then translate the content you typed in the editor with Shopify's Translate and Adapt app. To change the wording of a theme text, use Edit default theme content in the theme's ... menu in your theme library.
