Next product

A band the full width of the page at the foot of the product page, in the colors of the next product, its name set large.

WhereOnly on product templates.

What it is for

Lead visitors from one product to the next, as the next note at the end of an article does. The section belongs to the product template and reads the product of the page; the demo places it last, just before the footer.

How it works

The band shows Label (Next formula unless you write your own) and the name of the next product, set large, on the colors that product wears on its own page; the whole band is one link. The next product is the product's custom.next_product metafield, else the next one in Collection, in custom.primary_collection or in the product's first collection, from the start again with Loop around; only the first 50 products of the collection are read. With Show the next product off, or for a product without a next one, nothing is printed on your store; in the theme editor a note says why.

Content tips

  • Set custom.next_product on each product to choose the order of the range yourself (see Metafields).
  • Keep the section last in the product template, just before the footer.
