Header

Your logo or wordmark, the main menu with its mega menus, search, the account and the bag.

WhereHeader group only.

What it is for

The header of every page, with the announcement bar above it.

How it works

Logo type shows your logo image or your store name as a wordmark. The Menu is the desktop navigation; a top item opens a mega menu when a Mega menu block names it in Linked menu item. Its Layout can be an Index (the categories set large with their product count, the products of the category in view and a preview in the product color), Chapters, Links and images, Images only, Links only or Custom blocks.

On phones the menu opens as a sheet: its large links are the Main links menu, each with the number of products of its collection (the demo uses the shop menu), followed by Secondary links (the demo uses more). Search opens full screen, with your Popular products and your Search suggestions as words.

With Header behavior on Transparent at top, the header lies over a first section that allows it. As the page scrolls, it takes the surface of the section under it, with its text kept readable, hides on the way down and comes back on the way up.

Content tips

  • Create the menus main-menu, shop and more in your admin (see Menus).
  • Upload a white logo in Logo for dark backgrounds if your logo is dark.

Mega menu blocks

A mega menu is a panel that opens under a top item of the main menu on desktop. Add a Mega menu block to the Header, type the exact title of that menu item in Linked menu item and choose its Layout. On phones the menu sheet lists the Main links instead.

Mega menu

The panel itself. Layout builds it from the sub-links of the linked menu item: Index (the categories set large with their product count, the products of the category in view and a preview of the product in its colors, as on the demo Shop menu), Chapters (the sub-links as numbered chapters, as on the demo Rituals menu), Links and images, Images only or Links only; or from the blocks you add inside it, with Custom blocks.
