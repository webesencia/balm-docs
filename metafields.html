Metafields

Metafields give each product, variant, collection and article its own colors, cutout and texts. This page lists every metafield Balm Studio reads, with its key and its type.

Create each definition in your admin, under Settings, Custom data, then choose the owner (Products, Variants, Collections, Blog posts or Pages) and type the namespace and key exactly as shown: custom is the namespace. Then fill the values on each product, collection or article. A metafield left empty shows nothing, or the fallback described below.

The two colors

The dark color (custom.background_color) and the light color (custom.background_color_end) are the heart of the theme: from them it works out the light ground, the text, the dark ground and the paper of every page and section that shows the product, with the contrast checked. Pick the dark color deep enough to carry white text, and the light one as the color of the product itself.

The cutout

Upload a PNG of the product standing upright on a transparent background, cropped tight to the product, about 450 by 1100 pixels for a tube. It stands in the hero, the bands, the line-up and the product page. Without it the featured image of the product is used, which looks best when it is also a cutout.

Ratings

The Rating block of the product page, the quick view and Compare products read the standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count metafields first: review apps write them for you. Without them they read custom.rating and custom.rating_count, which you fill yourself. Without either, no rating is shown.

Connecting a setting to a metafield

A text setting of a block or a section can show a metafield instead of a fixed text: select the dynamic source icon next to the setting in the theme editor and pick the metafield. The definition has to exist first. The demo product template is already connected to the keys above for the passport, the How to use and Ingredients rows and the Texture section.

Metafields you type by key

A few settings are text fields where you type the namespace.key yourself instead of using the dynamic source icon.

WhereWhat to typeMetafield type

Product list metafields

The Products setting of the Product upsell block, of the Complementary products block and of the cart recommendations can be connected to a product metafield of type Product, set to accept a list of products, so each product carries its own selection. Products come from must then be on A manual selection.

Compare products rows

Each Row block of Compare products with Source on Metafield reads the product metafield you name in its Metafield setting, on every compared product. Type the name of the metafield, exactly as it shows in Settings, Custom data, Products (Skin type), or its key (custom.skin_type). The field takes no dynamic source on purpose: a connected metafield would give the value of the product of the page in every column. Every type is drawn as such: true or false as icons, a list as a sentence, a number with the row's Unit, a rating out of its scale, a color with its swatch, a reference by its title. A product without a value shows a hyphen. The other sources (Vendor, Product type, Tags, SKU, Barcode, Weight and Unit price) read the product's own fields.

Settings that cannot be connected

Shopify allows connections on colors, texts, rich texts, images, videos, links, pages, collections, products and metaobjects only. Sliders, checkboxes, dropdowns, the color scheme and Liquid code are the same for the whole template. When one product needs a different value for one of those, give it a second product template.
