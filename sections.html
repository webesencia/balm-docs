Settings most sections share

A handful of settings come back in almost every section. They work the same way everywhere, so they are explained once here.

Color scheme

Picks one of the schemes defined in Theme settings, Colors. The scheme paints the section background, its text and its buttons.

Surface

Most sections have a Surface setting that paints their background. Field, Deep and Paper follow the color of the page, so a section changes with the product it sits under:

  • Field: the light ground of the product color.
  • Deep: the dark version of the same color, with light text.
  • Paper: an almost white ground tinted with the color.
  • Color scheme: the section's color scheme instead of the product color.
  • Image: a picture behind the section, with the color scheme for the text.

Pages without a product color (the cart, a plain page, the 404) use the house pair of Theme settings, Tone.

Spacing, desktop and mobile

Sizes and spacings come in pairs: a label ending in (desktop) applies from 768 px wide, one ending in (mobile) applies below. Top and bottom padding set the space inside the section, above and below its content.

Text sizes are the exception: they are fluid. The (mobile) value applies on a screen 390 px wide, the (desktop) value from 1440 px, and the size moves smoothly in between, then Heading scale or Body scale multiplies it. See Theme settings, Typography. The announcement bar and the mega menu switch their text sizes at 1200 px instead, and decorative texts, such as the text behind cutouts of the Product section, keep a desktop and a mobile size off the scale.

Section width

Almost every section has a Section width setting, right after its color scheme. It sets how far the section's background, image or card reaches:

  • Theme default, the first option and the default: the Default section width of Theme settings, Layout, Full width with margins unless you change it there. Sections left on Theme default all change width at once from there.
  • Full width: from one edge of the screen to the other. Pictures and videos (Image, Image with text, Before and after, Video, Slideshow) run edge to edge with square corners; text sections keep their text to a readable width inside, and a grid, a table or a carousel keeps the side margin of a floating header, never more than the Section side margins.
  • Full width with margins: the whole screen less the Section side margins of Theme settings, Layout, one value on desktop and one on mobile.
  • Page width: stops at the Page width of Theme settings, Layout, with its side margin, so the section lines up with the product, collection and search pages.

Inset in Full width with margins or Page width, the frame of Before and after and the slides of the Slideshow take Corner radius: Theme setting (media), the media radius of Theme settings, Corner style, by default, or Custom, 0 to 40 px (square at 0), one value on Before and after and one per device on the Slideshow. In Full width they stay square, edge to edge.

The sections that can run under a transparent header, Product, Contact and Collection, keep their background (color, gradient or image) edge to edge in every width, never a card: Section width moves their content only, to the page width, to the Section side margins, or to the inside margin of Full width. The card of Contact stays inside that width.

The setting replaces the width settings that some sections used to have on their own (Before and after, Image, Image with text, Lookbook, Custom Liquid, Slideshow, Video and Apps).

The header over a section

With Header behavior set to Transparent at top (the default) or Adaptive in the Header section, the header can lie over the first section of a page, transparent, in the colors that suit what is under it. See Automatic header colors.

  • Hero, Slideshow, Video, Products showcase and Brand story hero carry Allow transparent header. When the section is first on the page, the header lies over it and starts transparent. The extra top padding settings keep your content clear of the header only while it is transparent.
  • Many other sections carry Header over this section, off by default: Contact, FAQ, Image, Page, Rich text, Multicolumn, Routine, Key ingredients, Key figures, Bento, Scroll-revealed text, Featured product, Before and after, Collection list, Compare products, Custom Liquid, Logo list, Blog, Blog post, 404, List of collections and Collection. Checked on the first section of the template, the section's background (color, gradient or image) runs up behind the announcement bar and the header, and its content starts under the header.
  • The Product section has neither. In its Product gradient background mode, Extend the background under the header plays that part: see Extend the gradient under the header.

Whatever their Section width, Contact, Collection and the Product section keep their background edge to edge, so it can run under the header; only their content moves in.

With Header behavior on Standard, these settings have no effect: the header keeps its card above the page.

Text colors

Most recent sections have a Text colors setting. Color scheme keeps the text of the section's scheme. Auto reads the real background of the section (its color, gradient or picture) and keeps the scheme's text when it reaches a contrast of 4.5:1 there, otherwise switches to white or black, for the text, icons, lines and buttons. Pick Auto when a section background makes the scheme's text hard to read.

Inner panel and text color

The About page sections and Contact place their content on an inner panel, the layer between the background and the text: Solid uses the color and opacity you set, Glass uses the shared glass recipe, Hidden removes it. Text color left on None lets the section work out a readable color from the background and the panel; pick a color only to force one.

Heading level

Search engines and screen readers expect exactly one main heading (H1) per page. Sections that often open a page (Hero, FAQ, Contact, Featured set, Store locator, Brand story hero) let you choose Main heading (H1) or a subheading level. The templates that open with one of them already set H1; keep H2 anywhere else. Product, collection, blog and page templates set their own H1.

Text left empty

Many text settings say Leave empty to use the translated default. Left empty, they show a built in text that is already translated into every language the theme ships (English, French, German, Italian and Spanish). Typing your own text replaces it in every language, so on a multilingual store either leave these fields empty or translate your text with the Translate & Adapt app. See Translations.

Spam protection notice

Shopify protects the contact and newsletter forms with hCaptcha, which must be disclosed. Balm Studio prints the required sentence, with its two links, under each form, which removes the hCaptcha badge from the corner of the page. The Spam protection notice settings only change its size, color and alignment.

Balm Studio sections

The sections of the Balm Studio demo store. Each one wears the color of the product it shows: the theme reads the two colors of the product (see Metafields, Product color) and works out the ground, the text and the deep panels from them, with the contrast checked on the server. While a section moves from one product to the next, the whole page glides from one color to the other. Every section reads correctly without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion see each step at once, without the glide.

The captures come from the demo store, on a desktop screen and on a phone.

Hero sequence

WhereAny template. Not available in the header or footer group.

The products of the home page, one after the other, as you scroll. Each Product block is one step: the product name sits large behind the cutout, its last line in front of it, and a bar at the bottom shows the name, the type and format, the first sentence of the tagline, the price, View formula and Add to bag. Scrolling lifts and turns each product away while the next one rises, and the color of the page follows, with a short pause on each product. Scroll length per step sets how long each product stays, on desktop and on phones; Object height sets the size of the cutout; Bar surface paints the bar. Without JavaScript each product is a still screen in its own color.

Typical use. Three to five products at the top of the home page, each with its two colors and its cutout.

The main section of each template: what a product, collection, cart or blog page shows. Most of them cannot be removed from their template, but their settings are yours.

Product

WherePart of the product template.

The product page: on one side a stage with the product name in layers around the cutout and Next formula, the name of the next product, which opens it; on the other a column of blocks (breadcrumb, title, rating, price, tagline, options, buy button, the product passport, collapsible rows, reviews and products that pair well). The whole page wears the product color, and choosing a variant that has its own colors recolors it. It is the main section of the product template, so it is not offered in Add section.

Its settings and every block are documented on Product page blocks.

Product recommendations

WhereOnly on product templates.

Product cards under the product page. Recommendation intent picks where they come from: Related (similar products) are generated by Shopify, Complementary (pairs well with) are the ones you curate in the Search & Discovery app, and Random from a collection shows products of the Collection you pick, in a new order on every visit. Fill up from the collection completes Shopify's recommendations from that collection when they are fewer than the maximum. Neither ever shows a product twice, nor the product of the page.

The cards sit in a grid or a carousel per device, with the arrows of Theme settings, Carousels, and Badges picks which badges they show.

Typical use. Four related products under every product page, with the custom badge showing on new formulas.

Sticky add to cart

WhereOnly on product templates.

A bar with the product thumbnail, title, selected variant, price and add to cart button that stays at the top or bottom of the screen, on desktop and mobile separately. By default it appears once the main buy button scrolls out of view. A variant that sells as a pre-order says Pre-order here too.

The bar exists only as this section, which the product template places below the Product section. Preview versions of Balm Studio also offered it as a Sticky add to cart block inside the Product section; that block has been removed.

Typical use. At the bottom of the screen on phones, where the main buy button quickly scrolls away.

Collection

WherePart of the collection template.

The collection page: a banner with the title, description and image, an optional row of collection links, filters and sorting, and the product grid. Filters come from the Search & Discovery app and can sit in a column, a drawer or a horizontal bar, whose drawer style is set in Theme settings, Filters. Pagination is page numbers, a Load more button or infinite scroll. Visitors can change the column count when you allow it (see Column selector), and Promo tile blocks take the place of a card on the first page. Badges picks the badges of the cards, and Text behind cutouts prints the product's title behind PNG and WebP pictures. It is the main section of the collection template, so it is not offered in Add section.

The banner shows, in this order, the collection's custom.banner_image metafield, the Banner image set in the section, or the collection's own image. Shopify keeps no focal point on a collection's image: pick the picture in the section or in the metafield to frame it at the focal point set in Files. See Metafields, Banner image.

Typical use. Filters in a column on desktop and a drawer on mobile, 24 products per page with a Load more button, color swatches on the cards.

Cart drawer

WherePart of every page. It is listed in the theme editor and cannot be removed or moved.

The panel that slides in when a product is added to the cart, or the short notification that replaces it. It has no settings of its own: its type, style, free shipping bar, recommendations, order note, discount code and checkout buttons are set in Theme settings, Cart, shared with the cart page.

The free shipping bar only displays progress toward the amount you type. It does not create free shipping or a discount: set the real rule in your admin (Settings, Shipping and delivery, or Discounts) and keep the two amounts the same.

Typical use. A floating glass drawer with a free shipping bar at 50 in your currency, three complementary suggestions in a carousel and the order note.

Mega menu blocks

A mega menu is a panel that opens under a top level item of the main menu. Add a Mega menu block to the Header section, type the exact title of that menu item in Linked menu item, then add the blocks below inside it. On phones the panel becomes an accordion in the mobile menu.

Mega menu

The panel itself: which menu item opens it, its layout, width, background and padding. Layout builds the panel from the sub-links of the linked menu item (Links and images, Images only or Links only) or from the blocks you add inside it (Custom blocks).

With Custom blocks, the blocks sit on one row of the panel and wrap onto a new row when they do not fit, exactly Block spacing (desktop) apart. Each block has a Width (desktop): Auto (its content), 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 2/3 or Fill (what the others leave). Horizontal alignment (desktop) and Vertical alignment (desktop) place them when they leave room. Media height (desktop) on Match tallest, the default, gives every block with a picture the height of the tallest one, text under the picture included, so every bottom lines up; Fixed uses the height you set.

In the mobile menu the links come first, then the cards (Promo, Image and each card of Image cards), all of the same width and Card ratio (mobile), laid out by Mobile cards layout: Carousel (one row that scrolls sideways, the next card showing), Grid (two columns) or Stack.
