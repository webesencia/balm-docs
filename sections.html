Sections

Balm Studio is built from sections you add, remove and reorder in the theme editor. Each section of the demo store has its own page; the sections of the first version of the theme are on More sections.

Home page

The sections of the demo home page, in their order.

  • Hero sequenceYour products one after the other at the top of the home page, each in its own color, as the visitor scrolls.
  • CampaignA photograph and a short text on a flat panel: the story of a season or of a launch.
  • Color libraryThe whole range at a glance: one tall band per product, each in its color.
  • Line-upYour products standing side by side on a ruler, each at its real height.
  • A day in colorA day told in steps, from morning to night, each with the product that fits it.
  • Routine builderShoppers build their routine, one product per step, and add it to the cart in one click.
  • ReviewsA summary of your reviews and up to three quotes from your customers, each linked to its product.
  • Hue finderA short quiz that leads a hesitant shopper to one product.
  • AtelierA wide photo of your place, then its address, its hours and how to visit.

Product page

The product template: the product itself is on Product page blocks.

  • TextureA photo of the product's texture, or its matter drawn in its color, with its texture, its skin feel and how to use it.
  • Next productA band the full width of the page at the foot of the product page, in the colors of the next product, its name set large.
  • Sticky add to cartA bar at the bottom of the product page, with the product and its button, once the page button has scrolled away.

Collection

The collection template.

  • CollectionThe collection page: a head with the title and a menu of collections, then the products as tiles or as an index.

About page

The page.about template.

  • ChaptersYour story in chapters, each on the color of the product it led to.
  • Ingredient atlasA wall of ingredient names: point at one to see what it does and which products contain it.
  • NumbersLarge figures with a line of text under each.

Contact page

The page.contact template.

  • ContactThe contact page: a heading, your details and the contact form.

Journal

The blog and blog post templates.

  • JournalThe blog as a journal: the newest article large, in the color of its product, then the others as rows.
  • Journal articleThe article page: the title in the color of the article product, the text, the product and the next article.

Every page

The header and footer groups, and the cart drawer.

  • HeaderYour logo or wordmark, the main menu with its mega menus, search, the account and the bag.
  • FooterThe newsletter, your menus, your store name set across the page and the legal lines.
  • Cart drawerThe cart, opened from the side as a sheet in the color of the last product added.

More sections

The sections of the first version of the theme, and the template sections the demo does not use, are on More sections.

Every section wears a color

Each product carries two colors, a dark and a light one, in its metafields (see Metafields). The theme works out six colors from them on the server, with the contrast of every text checked: the light ground (Field), the color itself (Tone), the text (Ink), a dark ground (Deep), a color for large text on Deep, and an almost white ground (Paper). A product page, a collection or a section that shows a product takes these colors, and the page glides from one color to the next as the visitor scrolls. Pages without a product color use the house pair of Theme settings, Tone. A product without its own colors takes a color of its own, derived from the house pair: the same on its page, its tile, the menu, the cart and every section that shows it, different from one product to the next, never gray.

The large name

Hero sequence, Color library, the product page and Featured product set the product's name large behind the product. Its lines come from Name lines in a Hero sequence block, else from the product's custom.name_lines metafield (a bar | at each break, four lines at most), else from its title, cut into Number of lines (Auto: three at most). Default layering brings lines in front of the product: Behind the product, Last line in front, First and last lines in front or All in front; in a Hero sequence block, Use custom layering and Line 1 in front to Line 4 in front choose line by line. Opacity of lines in front lets the product show through them. Name size Fixed follows the screen, Auto sizes the longest line to most of the width of the scene; Scale makes it smaller or larger, from 60 to 130 percent. Whatever the settings, no line runs off the screen or under the header, and the name keeps its size for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

Settings most sections share

Surface

Most sections have a Surface setting that paints their background:

  • Field: the light ground of the page color.
  • Deep: the dark ground of the same color, with light text.
  • Paper: an almost white ground tinted with the color.
  • Color scheme: the section's color scheme (see Theme settings, Colors) instead of the page color.
  • Image: a picture behind the section, with the color scheme for the text.

Some sections offer only some of these surfaces.

Section width

The sections that have Section width reach Page width, Full width with margins or Full width. Theme default follows Default section width of Theme settings, Layout, so every section left on it changes at once.

The header over a section

With Header behavior on Transparent at top in the Header, the header lies over the first section of a page when that section allows it. The demo sections that open a page (Hero sequence, Contact, Journal, Journal article and Chapters) allow it on their own. Many sections of More sections have Header over this section or Allow transparent header for the same purpose.

Desktop and mobile values

Sizes and spacings come in pairs: a label ending in (desktop) and one ending in (mobile). Text sizes are fluid between the two and are multiplied by Heading scale or Body scale of Theme settings, Typography. Use the device switch at the top of the editor to preview both.

Anchor and breadcrumbs

Anchor names a section so a menu link can point at it, for example /#lineup. Breadcrumbs on this page shows or hides the breadcrumb trail on one page, whatever Theme settings, Breadcrumbs says.

Empty content

An empty text prints nothing on your store, and a block that misses what it needs (a review without a quote, a step without a product, once the section has products) is left out. A section of products where you have chosen none shows the first products of your store, and a store without products shows Shopify's product placeholders there. In the theme editor such a block keeps its frame with a short note that says why it is hidden, so you can still select it.
