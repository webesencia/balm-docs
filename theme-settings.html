Theme settings

Theme settings apply to the whole store. Open them in the theme editor with the Theme settings icon (the gear) in the left bar. The groups below follow the order of the editor, and every label is written exactly as the editor shows it. The Default column gives the theme's built in value; the Balm Studio preset, which your store starts from, replaces the colors, fonts, corners, glass and typography values with its own.

Colors

Balm Studio organizes its colors in color schemes. A scheme is a set of seven colors designed to work together, and every section, popup and drawer picks one scheme with its own Color scheme setting. Edit a scheme here and every place that uses it follows.

The Balm Studio preset ships two schemes. Scheme 1 is the main one, white; Scheme 2 is its contrasting partner, near black. Add as many schemes as you need with Add scheme.

  • Background: the section background. Background gradient is optional and replaces it where set.
  • Text: body text, headings and icons.
  • Button / accent and Button label: the fill and the text of primary buttons.
  • Secondary background: the second surface of the scheme, used by cards, drawers, panels, inputs and secondary buttons. Balm Studio derives a raised tone and a hairline from it, so dark schemes keep visible layers.
  • Accent (links / highlights): links and highlighted details.

Keep the text and button colors readable on their background: aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text. Glass surfaces measure their own contrast and switch the label to black or white when needed, but plain surfaces use exactly the colors you set.

Typography

Two fonts drive the whole theme: the Heading font for headings and titles, and the Body font for paragraphs, forms and everything else. Both come from the Shopify font library, so they are served by Shopify and free to use on your store. The Balm Studio preset pairs Outfit 400 for headings with Inter for body text.

Type sizes. Balm Studio sets its text on one fluid scale. Every size has a mobile value, reached on a screen 390 px wide, and a desktop value, reached at 1440 px. In between, the size grows in a straight line; outside that range, it holds at the nearer value. On the scale, the product title runs from 32 to 44 px, section headings from 26 to 36 px, card titles from 15 to 17 px, body text from 15 to 16 px and small text from 12 to 13 px, each level with its own line height and letter spacing.

The text size settings of sections and blocks follow the same rule: the value of a setting ending in (mobile) applies at 390 px, the one ending in (desktop) at 1440 px, and the size moves smoothly between them. Their defaults sit on the scale, so a section you add matches the rest of the page. The announcement bar and the mega menu are the exceptions: they switch from one value to the other at the menu breakpoint, 1200 px, where the header changes menus. Decorative texts, such as the text behind cutouts of the product page or the two words across the Gallery banner, keep their own desktop and mobile sizes, off the scale.

Heading scale and Body scale (80 to 130%, 100% by default) multiply every size at once, the sizes set in sections and blocks included. Heading scale acts on headings, titles and product card titles; Body scale on body text, buttons, labels and prices. Set Heading scale to 110% and every heading of the store grows by a tenth, without opening a single section.

Three shared recipes follow. Headings sets the case, the slant and the weight of every display heading. Button labels sets the case, weight, letter spacing and slant of every button. Labels and small text sets the case of eyebrows, badges, prices, filters, variant pills, the announcement bar, the footer links and the main menu. Because the recipes are global, a section title, a product card and a page title always speak with the same voice.

Not every family offers every weight or an italic. When a family lacks the face you ask for, the browser uses the closest one it has, or slants an upright face. Outfit, the heading font of the Balm Studio preset, has no italic at all: with Heading style set to Italic, its letters are slanted by the browser. For true italic headings, pick a heading font whose family includes italics.

Layout

Page width sets how wide the content of the product, collection and search pages can grow, together with the Featured collection, Product recommendations and Recently viewed sections.

  • Standard, the default: 1400 px at most, centered.
  • Wide: 1600 px at most.
  • Full width with margins: the whole screen, less the side margins of the header. The page content, the breadcrumbs and the footer then line up with the logo and the icons of the header, a floating header included.

Product card images are served at the size the chosen width draws them, so cards stay sharp on a wider page.

Default section width is the width of every section whose Section width is Theme default, the first option of that setting and the default of each section: Page width, Full width with margins (the default) or Full width. Change it here and every section left on Theme default follows; a section set to one of the three widths keeps its own. A section's Page width option stops at the Page width chosen above. See Section width.

Section side margins (desktop), 0 to 160 px (40 px by default), and Section side margins (mobile), 0 to 48 px (16 px by default, under 768 px wide), set the space on each side of a section in Full width with margins, for every section. The Balm Studio preset sets them to 60 px and 20 px.

Corner style

Four sliders set the maximum corner radius of the whole theme: Buttons and pills, Cards and panels, Form fields and Images and media. Each value is a ceiling: elements designed with smaller corners keep them, and 0 gives square corners everywhere. Section settings that carry their own radius, such as the floating header or the cart drawer, are capped by these values too.

The Balm Studio preset sets buttons and form fields to 12 px, cards and media to 20 px.

Buttons

One recipe for every button of the theme, primary and secondary. Size (Small, Medium by default, or Large) sets the height, the padding and the text size on desktop and on mobile, one step down or up the type scale from Medium. A Small button keeps a tap area of 44 px around it. Border width (1 or 2 px) applies to outline buttons, and Font weight (Regular, Medium or Semibold) to every label.

The case, letter spacing and slant of the labels are set in Typography, the corners in Corner style. A few buttons can step out of the shared size: the Buy buttons block of the product page (Button size), the quick add button of the cards (Size in Product cards) and the button of a Products showcase spotlight. Each offers Follow buttons, which keeps this setting.

Product cards

One look for the product cards of the Collection, Search, Featured collection, Product recommendations, Recently viewed and Lookbook sections, set once here.

  • Sizes. The title and price sizes, on desktop and on mobile. The defaults sit on the type scale: titles 17 px on desktop and 15 px on mobile, prices 16 and 14 px, with the compare-at price at 85% of the price. Heading scale applies to the titles, Body scale to the prices. The cards of the 404 page follow them too; the Complementary products block of the product page keeps its own title size.
  • Image. Hover effect decides what a mouse does to the picture. With Second image on hover turned on in the section, Fade crossfades the product's two images and Swap changes them at once. Zoom enlarges the image, and crossfades to the second one when it is on.
  • Quick add button. The style of the card button (Add to cart, Choose options, Shop now): Solid, Outline, Text link, Glass or Icon only (a bag icon), Auto or Full width, below the product details or on the image (shown on hover on desktop, always on touch screens), and the button colors of the color scheme or your own. Its size follows Buttons unless you pick one here. Where the button shows is set per section, with Quick add.

Text behind cutouts on the cards

The product's title, or its custom.tagline metafield, can stand as a large decorative text behind the picture of each card of a collection grid, seen through the transparent areas of PNG and WebP images. JPG images never get it. The Collection section decides where it shows, with Text behind cutouts, Show on desktop and Show on mobile; this group sets its look for every card at once: Text, Font, Font weight, Text case, Letter spacing, Line height, Lines, the vertical and horizontal position per device, the Text color (the scheme text, the product's custom.background_color_end metafield or a custom color), Text opacity (15% by default) and Text style, Solid or Outline with its thickness. The text is hidden from screen readers. The product page has its own version of it: see Text behind cutout images.

Product badges

Four badges can sit on the product cards, in quick view, in Compare products and on the product page. Sale shows automatically when the compare-at price is above the price, Sold out when the product can no longer be bought. New is off by default: set New badge to Product tag and name the tag, or to Created in the last X days and set the number of days. Custom prints the product's custom.badge_label metafield, read directly. This group sets their position, shape and colors once for the whole store.

A product can give its Custom badge its own colors through two optional Color metafields, custom.badge_color (background) and custom.badge_text_color (text). Left empty, the badge uses Custom background and Custom text from this group. With a background and no text color, the text turns dark or light by itself, at a contrast of at least 4.5:1. See Give one product its own badge colors.

On the product page, turn on Badges on media in the Product section, or add a Badge block. Every section that shows product cards has its own Badges setting (All, Automatic only, Custom only or None) to keep some of them, or none, in that section: see Choose the badges of each section. See Product badges for the full walkthrough.

Pre-order turns itself on for a variant whose quantity is tracked, whose stock is at 0 and that has Continue selling when out of stock checked. The add to cart button, the sticky add to cart bar and the quick add button then read Pre-order, or the label you type here. See Pre-order.

Cart

The whole cart is set up here, once: the drawer, the notification and the cart page share these settings, so the Cart drawer section has none of its own.

  • Cart type: Drawer (the default) slides a panel in after each add, Page opens the cart page, Notification confirms the add with the line just added, View cart and Check out.
  • Drawer and notification: Style Solid (the color scheme's background) or Glass (the Glass effect over the whole drawer, top to bottom). Floating drawer, on by default, detaches the drawer and the notification from the screen edges by the margins you set for desktop and mobile, with the corners of Corner style. Panel width, paddings, heading and title sizes, thumbnail size and Line density (Comfortable, or Compact for a smaller thumbnail and tighter lines, in the drawer and on the cart page) follow.
  • Colors: Text colors on Color scheme keeps the scheme's text; on Auto, the cart reads its real background, glass included, and keeps the scheme text when it reaches 4.5:1, otherwise white or black.
  • Free shipping bar: progress toward the Free shipping threshold you type, in your store currency and converted for visitors who shop in another one. Nothing shows while the threshold is empty. The bar only displays progress: create the matching free shipping rate or discount in your admin and keep the two amounts the same. Measure progress on the subtotal before cart discounts, which is what Shopify's own free shipping rates use, or on the total after discounts.
  • Recommendations: Shopify's complementary products of the cart's lines first, then related ones, or a manual selection, never a product already in the cart, as a carousel or a list, with quick add.
  • Cart content: the order note, the discount code field (off by default; codes are checked by Shopify as soon as they are applied, then listed and removable), a You save line, the taxes and shipping note, a shipping notice and a promotion note on each line.
  • Checkout: the accelerated checkout buttons in the drawer and on the cart page, both on by default, and the payment icons of your store.
  • Empty cart: a message, a button to the collection you choose and an optional selection of products.

Gift wrapping adds its checkbox to the drawer and the cart page from its own group.

Glass effect

One frosted glass recipe shared by every glass surface of the theme: the glass header and mega menu, the announcement bar in Glass style, glass buttons, the popups and the cart drawer when they use a glass card. Set the Tint, Fill opacity, Blur and Border opacity once and every surface moves together.

Glass on phones decides what happens on screens under 768 px wide: Frosted keeps the blur, Near opaque swaps it for an almost solid fill that is lighter to scroll on older phones. Browsers without backdrop blur and visitors who ask for reduced transparency always get the near opaque fill.

Glass elements is a second, lighter recipe for the small pieces set to Glass inside the sections: cards, FAQ questions, form fields, buttons and the round controls that sit over a picture (carousel arrows, Lookbook hotspots, the media controls of the product gallery, the video controls of the Hero). Element fill opacity, Element blur and Element border opacity set it; the tint is the one above. These elements turn nearly solid for visitors who ask for less transparency. See Glass.

Loader

The Page loader: an optional loading screen shown while pages open, with your logo, a progress bar or a spinner, and an exit animation. It is the theme's only loader, and it covers everything on the page, the header and the announcement bar included. See Page loader.

The progress bar has three settings of its own, shown with the Logo and progress bar and Progress bar only layouts: Bar ends (Square, or Rounded, the default, which rounds the track and the fill), Bar thickness (1 to 6 px, 2 by default) and Bar animation (None, Smooth by default, or Shimmer).

Preview versions of Balm Studio had a second loader here: a loading indicator drawn on the hero while its video loaded, with seven settings (Style, Loader image, Animation, Size (desktop), Size (mobile), Color and Custom color). It has been removed with those settings. The hero now shows its cover image while its video loads, sharp or blurred, as set by While the video loads in the Hero section.
