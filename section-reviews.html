Reviews

A summary of your reviews and up to three quotes from your customers, each linked to its product.

WhereAny template. Not available in the header or footer group.

What it is for

Show what customers say, under the products they talk about. Nothing is written for you: the summary, the quotes, the names and the ratings are yours.

How it works

Summary is a line you write, for example your average rating and the number of reviews, with a link to all your reviews. Each Review block has a Quote, an Author, a Rating and a Product: the quote shows its stars, and the author line ends with a small square in the color of that product and a link to it. Columns is set per device. A review without a quote is hidden on your store.

Content tips

  • Quote real customers, in their own words.
  • Write in Summary only what your reviews support.
