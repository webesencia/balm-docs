Routine builder

Shoppers build their routine, one product per step, and add it to the cart in one click.

WhereAny template. Not available in the header or footer group.

What it is for

A large paragraph introduces the routine, with small cutouts of products set in the line; under it, one column per step lets shoppers choose a product, and the total of their choice is added in one go.

How it works

In Paragraph, [1], [2] and [3] become the cutouts of Inline product 1 to 3. Each Step block has a Label and two or three Products, written as words; choosing one paints the column in that product's color and brings its cutout in. The summary lists the chosen products, then your Offer text, then the real total of their prices: the theme computes no discount. The button adds the chosen products together and opens the cart. Without JavaScript each step is a group of radio buttons in a real add to cart form.

Content tips

  • To offer a discount on the routine, create it in your admin (Discounts) and describe it in Offer text. See The routine discount.
  • Pick products that are in stock: a step starts on its first available product.
