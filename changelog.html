Changelog

What changed in each version of Balm Studio. The version installed in your store is shown in your theme library, under the theme name.

All notable changes to the Balm Studio theme are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning. The version installed in a store is shown in the theme library, next to the theme name.

1.0.0 (2026-10-31)

Initial release.

Design

  • Tone on tone: each product carries a tone, two colors stored in the product metafields custom.background_color and custom.background_color_end. The theme derives six colors from them on the server (field, tone, ink, deep, deeptone and paper), checks every text pair for contrast and corrects it before the page is sent, so pages are right and readable without JavaScript. A product page, a collection, the cart and the hero take the tone of what they show, and move smoothly from one tone to the next.
  • Headings in Gloock, body text in Instrument Sans, one easing curve for every movement, square corners, no glass, shadow or faded text.
  • Sliders use the words Previous and Next and a counter ("02 / 05") instead of drawn arrows and dots. Filled buttons, underlined links, accordions with a turning "+" and underlined fields are shared by the whole theme.
  • Visitors who ask for less motion get every state at once, with nothing moving while they scroll; the Hero sequence then sets its products one under the other.
  • Large titles keep the room their descenders need above the text under them.
  • The large name behind the product (Hero sequence, Color library, the product page, Featured product): Name lines in a Hero sequence block (a bar at each break, four lines at most), Number of lines for a cut title, Default layering (behind, last line, first and last lines or all lines in front of the product) and, in a Hero sequence block, line by line; Opacity of lines in front; Name size Fixed or Auto with a Scale from 60 to 130 percent. No line runs off the screen or under the header. The defaults keep the previous look.
  • Journal article: in the left margin, the meta column (category, reading time, date, Back to the blog, Copy link) stands against the text, sticky beside the product card, from 1280px; one line above the text on smaller screens. Copy link announces Link copied to screen readers. Each part has its setting.
  • A product without its own colors takes a color of its own, derived from the house pair: the same on its page, its tile, the mega menu, the cart and every section that shows it, different from one product to the next, never gray. Its own colors always come first.

Preset

  • Balm Studio: a house tone, Gloock and Instrument Sans, the demo store's templates and header and footer groups, with no image, product, collection or metafield of the demo. Sections and blocks added in the theme editor come with example texts that say how to use them, in the language of the theme editor.
  • Without a product chosen in them, Hero sequence, Color library, Line-up, A day in color, Routine builder and Hue finder show the store's own products, in the order of the All products collection, as many as each has places for; a store without products shows Shopify's product placeholders there, on the store too. A day in color then takes each step's label and line from its product.

Theme settings

  • Tone: the house pair of colors, used where no product sets the tone.
  • Colors, Typography and Layout (page width, default section width, side margins).
  • Buttons: Size (Small, Medium, Large), Border width and Font weight, for every button of the theme, filled buttons included. A small button keeps a 44 px tap area.
  • Tiles, Product badges, Pre-order, Quick view, Cart, Gift wrapping, Back to top, Age verifier, Favicon, Social media, Social media sharing, Breadcrumbs, Search and Search engines.

Sections

  • A section or a block that expects an image keeps its frame with Shopify's placeholder drawing until an image is chosen, on the store as in the theme editor, in the colors of its section: Campaign, Atelier, Image, Chapters, Logo list, the cards of the mega menu and the Journal's featured article, as already Hero, Slideshow, Image with text, Lookbook and the others.
  • Every section has a Surface setting: Field (the page's tone, the default), Deep, Paper, Color scheme or Image. Text on each surface reads at 4.5:1 at least.
  • Home and content: Hero sequence (products that rise and turn as the page scrolls, the page following their tone), Hero, Campaign, Color library, A day in color, Routine builder (with an optional discount shown on the total), Reviews, Atelier, Line-up, Hue finder, Texture (the product's texture photo, custom.texture_image, else five renderings of its matter, gel, cream, balm, fluid and serum, picked from its texture or forced), Ingredient atlas, Chapters, Numbers, Scroll-revealed text, Marquee, Rich text (with a Statement style and Product inline blocks that set a small product in the sentence), Image (Reveal on scroll, on by default), Slideshow (a counter on phones from five slides), Featured product, Featured collection, Collection list, Key figures, Key ingredients, FAQ, Contact, Journal, Journal article, Newsletter, Testimonials, Lookbook, Video and more: 74 sections in all. The sections of the first look that the demo store does not use stay available, among them Contact classic and Footer classic.
  • Section width on every section: Page width, Full width with margins or Full width, the background always running edge to edge.

Product page

  • A scene with the product's name in layers behind the product cut out (custom.cutout, else its photo), a buy column, a passport of the product's facts, a sticky add to cart bar with the words of the page's button, and the variant's own tone. The scene is the gallery: the product's other media follow by swipe, mouse drag, trackpad, the arrow keys or the Previous and Next arrows on each side of the counter, with zoom, video and 3D; on phones the next media shows at the edge, and a variant brings its own image. On desktop the scene fills the screen under the header, the product and its name centered in it, the zoom and the counter centered under the product.
  • Next product: a band the full width of the page at the foot of the product page, in the colors of the next product (custom.next_product, else the next one in the collection), its name set large, the whole band one link; Show the next product and Label in the section.
  • Ratings from the standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count metafields, else from custom.rating and custom.rating_count, on the product page, in the quick view, in Compare products and, when turned on, on the collection tiles.
  • Reviews with Review blocks: the review of the page's product shows first, the others name the product they are about.
  • Page transitions: from a collection tile to the product page, the name and the product move into place (View Transitions, where the browser supports them).

Collection, search and cart

  • Collection tiles on the product's field, an Index view in rows, filters and sorting, and, when turned on, the rating and the color swatches of each product on its tile. An All tile fills the last row of a collection and links to all products.
  • Search in a full screen field, products before pages and articles.
  • Cart drawer and cart page: a free delivery bar in segments, a suggestion, the order note, discount codes, gift wrapping, an empty bag with its own sentence and link, and a toast when a product is added.

Header and menus

  • The header takes the surface of the section under it, hides going down and comes back going up. Mega menus as an index of categories with a product preview and an All entry, as chapters (each linked to the menu item's sub-link at its place), or with custom blocks; a full screen menu sheet on phones, with its own main links. A link to a section of the page (its Anchor) closes the menu and scrolls to the section, under the header.
  • The footer sets the store name from one gutter to the other, whatever its length.

Journal

  • Each article takes the colors of its product (custom.product) and shows its reading time, typed in custom.reading_time or worked out from its length. The text stands in a centred column, the product beside it from 1280px and under it below, with a reading bar at the top of the screen (Show the reading bar, on by default).

Accessibility and performance

  • No axe violation (critical, serious or moderate) on the home, product, collection, search, cart, contact, journal, article and about pages; a visible focus everywhere, keyboard access to every control.
  • Lighthouse on the demo store: performance averages of 86 on mobile and 99 on desktop, accessibility 100.

Languages

  • English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, for the storefront and the theme editor.
