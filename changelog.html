Changelog

What changed in each version of Balm Studio. The version installed in your store is shown in your theme library, under the theme name.

All notable changes to the Balm Studio theme are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning. The version installed in a store is shown in the theme library, next to the theme name.

1.0.0 (2026-10-13)

Initial release.

Preset

  • Balm Studio: clean and neutral. A white scheme, a near black scheme and two violet schemes, Outfit for headings (400) and Inter for body text, sentence case headings and buttons, small buttons, softly rounded corners (12 px buttons, 20 px cards), a white frosted glass and pages in Full width with margins.
  • The preset carries four color schemes, a font pairing, corner radii, a glass recipe and typography recipes. Every text measures a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
  • The templates and the header and footer groups follow the demo store's layout: the same sections, in the same order, with the same style settings, and no image, product, collection or metafield of the demo.
  • Sections and blocks added in the theme editor come with example texts that say how to use them, in the language of the theme editor (English, French, German, Italian or Spanish): they are the default values of their settings, which Shopify translates.

Theme settings

  • Color schemes with seven roles: background, optional background gradient, text, button, button label, secondary background and accent.
  • Heading and body fonts from the Shopify font library, and three shared typography recipes: headings (case, style, weight), button labels (case, letter spacing, style), labels and small text (case).
  • Type sizes: one fluid type scale for the whole theme, from 390 px to 1440 px wide (product title 32 to 44 px, section headings 26 to 36, card titles 15 to 17, card prices 14 to 16, body text 15 to 16, small text 12 to 13, footer column titles 13 to 14), with line height and letter spacing per level. Every size setting of the sections and blocks is fluid too, its mobile value at 390 px and its desktop value at 1440 px, and its default sits on the scale. The announcement bar and the mega menu keep their switch at the menu breakpoint. Heading scale and Body scale (80 to 130 %) multiply every heading size and every body size, section settings included.
  • Corner style: four maximum radii, for buttons, cards, form fields and media.
  • Buttons, for every button of the theme, primary and secondary: Size (Small, Medium, Large) sets the height, padding and text size on desktop and on mobile, one step of the type scale down or up from Medium, the drawing of each button; Border width of the outline buttons (1 or 2 px); Font weight (Regular, Medium, Semibold), moved from Typography with its value. A Small button keeps a 44 px tap area around it.
  • Glass effect: one frosted recipe (tint, fill opacity, blur, border opacity) shared by every glass surface, with a Frosted or Near opaque choice on phones. Glass elements: its own fill opacity, blur and border opacity for the cards, questions, fields and buttons set to Glass in the sections, which turn nearly solid for visitors who ask for less transparency.
  • Section backgrounds on most sections: solid color, two color gradient, image with an overlay, or radial halo, connectable to shop metafields, with a fallback color. The gradient takes the Linear, Radial glow (a halo of the lighter color placed and sized by the halo settings) and Aurora styles; Grain textures any background; an image background takes a mobile image; every image shows at its focal point, and the header reads the tone of each style where it really sits. Backgrounds: Animate auroras slowly, off by default and never with reduced motion. The radial halo now uses its desktop size on desktop.
  • Carousels: one look for the arrows and dots of every carousel (product gallery, product recommendations, testimonials): arrow style (Solid, Outline, Glass, Icon only), shape (Square, Rounded, Circle), size on desktop and on mobile, icon (Arrow, Chevron), colors (Auto, from the section around the carousel, or Custom), and dots or bars. Glass arrows carry a halo of the tone opposite to the arrow, like the other glass controls, so they read over a light or a dark photo. Carousels step one card at a time, drag with a mouse, follow the keyboard and stay still under reduced motion.
  • Social media: a URL for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Snapchat, Threads, Tumblr, Vimeo and LinkedIn. Only the networks with a URL show, each with its icon, opening in a new tab, named for screen readers with the store's name ("Balm on Instagram"): in the footer's Social block, in the Socials section, and in the header on desktop with Show social media icons.
  • Breadcrumbs, favicon, social sharing image, search scope, and a noindex option for search results pages. On product pages the breadcrumb and its structured data show the product's collection (Show the collection): the one the visitor came through (the cards of collection grids and of the featured collection link through it), or the product's custom.primary_collection metafield (Collection reference), or its first collection other than all and frontpage.
  • Product cards: title and price sizes on desktop and on mobile, shared by the cards of the collection, search, featured collection, product recommendations and recently viewed sections.
  • Layout: page width for the product, collection and search pages and the featured collection, product recommendations and recently viewed sections. Standard (1400 px), Wide (1600 px) or Full width with margins (the preset's choice), which lines up the page content, the breadcrumbs and the footer with the header's logo and icons (50 px from 1200 px, 20 px below, plus the side margin of a floating header). Product card images are sized for the chosen width.
  • Layout: Default section width (Page width, Full width with margins, the default, or Full width), taken by every section whose Section width is Theme default, and the side margins of Full width with margins, desktop (0 to 160 px, 40 px by default) and mobile (0 to 48 px, 16 px by default), the page's side margins.
  • Section width in every page section, after its color scheme: Theme default (the default, Default section width of Layout), Full width (the background or the picture from one edge of the screen to the other, text kept to a readable width), Full width with margins (the side margins of Layout; the frame of Before and after and of Video and the picture of Image with text span it, the screen less those margins) or Page width (the page width of Layout, where Video keeps its Maximum width and the frame of Before and after lines up with the content of the other sections; inset, that frame takes Corner radius, Theme setting (media) by default or Custom). The visible container of every section follows it: its box when it paints a background, otherwise its grid, table, carousel, panel or media, on the same edges as the sections around it in the same width, a grid or a table keeping in Full width the side margin of a floating header, never more than that of Full width with margins; a paragraph keeps its reading width inside. Video and Newsletter keep their Maximum width (desktop) in Page width, the only width that shows it. The sections that can run under a transparent header (Main product, Contact, Main collection) keep their background edge to edge in every width, never a card: Section width moves their content only (the page width, the side margins of Full width with margins, or the inside margin of Full width). The preset's templates use Theme default, except the sections that run edge to edge (Hero, Slideshow, Products showcase, Featured set, Lookbook, Marquee, Password page), in Full width. A Slideshow inset by Section width takes Corner radius, Theme setting (media) by default or Custom per device (0 to 40 px, square at 0).

Header, footer and popups

Once the page scrolls, a sticky floating card blurs the page that passes under it across the whole width, its side margins included, and fades out just under the card.
  • Header behavior, one setting: Standard (the card and its color scheme, always), Transparent at top (the default: transparent over a first section that allows it, in the colors that suit that section's tone, then the card on scroll) or Adaptive (transparent or glass at every scroll position, on every page, its colors and logo following the section under it). Colors over dark backgrounds (a color scheme with light text) and a logo for dark backgrounds; over light backgrounds the header keeps its own scheme and main logo. Background when scrolled (Glass or Transparent) in Adaptive only. The tone needs no setting in the sections: the header reads sixteen points on its middle line and the first background painted under each (a color, a gradient by its stops, a product gradient, a picture where it is drawn), and the color or surface that covers most of its width decides. Over a picture or a video, the text laid over it gives the tone, else a thumbnail of the part under the header, overlay included. It reads again as the page scrolls, as a slide changes and as the products showcase wipes to the next spotlight. An open menu, search or drawer, a popup or anything fixed to the screen never counts, and the standard colors return while a menu is open. The script loads only with Transparent at top or Adaptive.
  • Dropdowns of the main menu without any block: every picture in the same landscape 3:2 frame, filled at its focal point (a collection's or product's image, a blog post's image, a page's custom.image metafield), with the media radius of Theme settings and the title right under it.
  • Mega menu block, linked to a top level item: Layout Links and images (the item's sub-links in columns, Link columns and Links width, and image cards beside them), Images only, Links only, or Custom blocks (link columns, images, image cards, products with their tagline, promos and banners). Media height (desktop): Natural, Match tallest (the default) or Fixed with its Height (desktop), the blocks with a picture (Promo, Image, Image cards, Products) all as tall, the text under a picture counted so every bottom lines up, the pictures cropped at their focal point; the top of the Link column's heading lines up with the top of the pictures. In the mobile menu the links come first, then the cards (Promo, Image and each card of Image cards), all of one width, of Card ratio (mobile) and with the media corners of Theme settings, in Mobile cards layout: Carousel (the default, one row that scrolls with the next card showing, Carousel card width (mobile); the row is the only part of the menu that scrolls sideways, the links above it never move), Grid (two columns) or Stack (full width), Card spacing (mobile); 16 px between the item, the links, the cards and the next item. Link column: a menu (the header's main menu by default), All links or Only the links under a Parent link (by default the item the mega menu opens from), Show heading and a heading that defaults to the parent link's title (in the mobile menu a heading that repeats the item lists the links at once, without a second accordion), second and third levels with a Sub-link indent, underline on hover and on the current page, Heading, Link and Sub-link sizes for desktop and mobile (by default the header navigation's sizes); Group spacing (between the links of the first level, each with the links under it) and Link spacing (between the links under them), for desktop and mobile; Show thumbnails (desktop, mobile, off by default), the picture of each product and collection link before it, Thumbnails for (Products and collections, Products only, Collections only), with Thumbnail size (desktop, mobile) and Thumbnail corner radius, the other links of the list keeping its room so the texts line up; Show heading (desktop) and Show heading (mobile), the heading being an accordion in the mobile menu (without it the links show at once). Image: Image width, ratio (Fixed height included) and corner radius, as for Image cards, cropped at the focal point, a caption, a subtitle and a link label under the picture or on it; nothing on the store without a picture. Promo: a picture, a heading, a text and a link label, under the picture or on it. Image cards: Automatic (the picture of the collection, product with its price, blog post or page each sub-link points to, up to four) or Custom cards (image, title, subtitle, link, button label), cropped at the focal point; ratio (Original, Square, Portrait 4:5, 3:4 and 2:3, Landscape 4:3, 3:2 and 16:9, Fixed height), card width, corner radius (the theme's media radius or custom), spacing, hover effect (Zoom, Fade, None); text below the picture or on it; title and subtitle sizes; button style. Text on the picture, for Image, Promo and Image cards: Position on the image among nine places and Padding on the image, on desktop and on mobile; Text backdrop None, Soft gradient (from the text's side, fading toward the middle of the picture) or Soft glow (a blurred halo behind the text), only where the text is, with Backdrop color, Backdrop intensity (0 to 80 %) and Backdrop size; Text shadow; Text color on the image (white on a Soft gradient at 35 % by default); Heading weight and Heading case on desktop and on mobile, with the heading sizes. Every picture of the panel (Image, Promo, Image cards, Products, Banner) offers renditions up to its source's width and is loaded at the width it is really drawn, cropping included, up to twice that width on a dense screen. Panel width Full width, Page width or a maximum width, and Panel background Same as the header, Solid or Glass.
  • Country and language selectors in the header: a globe before the search, account and cart icons, alone or followed by the current language and currency codes, that opens a panel with the countries and their currency (with a search field above ten countries) and the languages. Accessibility

  • A skip link, landmarks and headings on every template, one h1 per template, visible focus rings and 24 px touch targets.
  • Focus kept inside the popups, the drawers, quick view and the age verifier, and live regions for variant changes and for the products added by Load more.
  • Carousels with tab semantics and pause controls, a pause button on the marquee, and a before and after handle usable from the keyboard.
  • Reduced motion respected: no video autoplay, no turning badge, instant scrolling and a still loader.
  • Glass button labels switch to black or white when their contrast would fall below the floor.
  • Every image has an alt attribute: the image's alt text, or an empty one when it has none (decorative). Routine steps are a real list. The previous and next product links keep a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
  • Shopify's cookie banner keeps the look set in the admin: the theme no longer restyles it.
  • Theme editor labels use Shopify's vocabulary and American spelling: heading, carousel, color, center.

Performance

  • Stylesheets scoped per section and block, subset by page, and feature scripts loaded only on the pages where the feature renders.
  • The hero video loads after the load event and the browser's first idle moment, over its cover, which paints first. The product page background no longer moves the layout while the page loads (its aurora layer starts at the top of the section).
  • Store links in scripts and fallbacks come from Shopify's routes, so a store with a language or a market in its URL (/fr) keeps it.
  • In the first two places of a template, a background picture and its 32 px blurred rendition load first, with a high priority, so the flat color never shows; picture covers (hero, store locator, gallery banner, featured set, password, collection banner) load at once with their blurred rendition under them.
  • One image per device on the slideshow, eager loading reserved for the first section, the hero video loaded after the page by default, and the animation library loaded on demand.
  • Glass surfaces fall back to a near opaque fill under reduced transparency, and on phones when the merchant picks Near opaque.
