Hero sequence

Your products one after the other at the top of the home page, each in its own color, as the visitor scrolls.

WhereAny template. Not available in the header or footer group.

What it is for

Open the store on the products themselves. Three to five products work best: each one fills the screen in turn, with its name set large around its cutout and a bar to buy it.

How it works

Each Product block is one step. The product name is set in layers behind the cutout, its last line in front of it, and a bar at the bottom shows the name, the type and format, the first sentence of the tagline, the price, View formula and Add to bag. Scrolling lifts the product away while the next one rises, and the whole page glides to the next color, with a short pause on each product.

Scroll length per step sets how long each product stays, on desktop and on phones; Object height sets the size of the cutout; Bar surface paints the bar in Field, Deep or Paper. Without JavaScript, and for visitors who ask for reduced motion, the products stand one under the other, each a still screen in its own color, and the page scrolls on to the next section.

Content tips

  • Give each product its two colors, its cutout and its name lines (see Metafields): the step uses all three.
  • Write a tagline whose first sentence stands alone: the bar shows only that sentence.
  • A block without a product is left out of the store.
