Collection

The collection page: a head with the title and a menu of collections, then the products as tiles or as an index.

WherePart of the collection template.

What it is for

Every collection page. Each tile wears the color of its product, and the page takes the color of the tile in view as visitors scroll.

How it works

The Menu of Collection navigation shows a menu of collections as words beside the title, the current one underlined: the demo uses a menu named shop-filters (All, Cleanse, Treat, Hydrate, Protect). Default view on desktop and Default view on mobile choose Grid, tiles in the color of each product, or Index, rows that preview each product as you point at it; Let visitors switch between Grid and Index adds the two words to the tools. The products follow the sort order of the collection set in your admin. Filters come from Shopify's Search and Discovery app.

Show the product rating prints the rating of each product above its name, with the number of reviews (see Product ratings). Show color swatches draws the values of its color option as swatches, each a link to its variant. Both are off on the demo.

Show an All tile closes the grid with a tile on the background of the page, its first word set large like the names of the tiles, that links to all products: All tile collection (empty: /collections/all) and All tile label (All formulas on the demo). It fills the empty cells of the last row, for the column count of each device and the one a visitor picks, and hides when the row is full; it never shows on its own collection.

Content tips

  • Give each collection its two colors (custom.background_color and custom.background_color_end) to tint its head.
  • Set the order of the products in your admin (Products, Collections, Sort): the page keeps it.
